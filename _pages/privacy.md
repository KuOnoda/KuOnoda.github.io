This personal academic website uses Google Analytics 4 only if you choose **Allow analytics**. It helps the site owner understand which pages are visited. There is no public visitor counter; the analytics dashboard is restricted to the site owner.

If you allow analytics, Google processes page-view and technical information (such as your browser and device), and receives your IP address when your browser connects to Google. Google Analytics does not log or store individual IP addresses. First-party analytics cookies distinguish browsers and visits; this site limits their lifetime to 90 days. These figures are estimates, not a complete count of every visitor.

The site sends page addresses without query strings or fragments and only the referring website's origin. It does not send form contents, names or email addresses to Analytics. Advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled. Analytics data is processed by Google under its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Analytics data safeguards](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245).

**Your choice:** Rejecting analytics does not affect access to this website. No Google Analytics requests are made before you allow it. Your choice is saved locally in this browser. Use **Analytics settings** in the footer to change your choice or withdraw consent; rejecting removes this site's Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop the loaded tracker. Withdrawal does not erase data already processed. Clearing browser storage resets your choice. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals are respected and override permission.

Analytics is disabled on local and preview versions. Visitors who decline, block analytics or use privacy signals are not counted. Hosting by GitHub Pages and any external sites you choose to open have their own privacy practices.
