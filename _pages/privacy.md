**No visitor analytics is currently active on this website.** Google Analytics is disabled, and the site does not load the Google Analytics tracking script or display an analytics consent prompt. When you revisit, the site removes its previous Google Analytics cookies from this browser.

Google Analytics was briefly tested during setup on November 5, 2026, with an opt-in prompt. Google may retain analytics data already received during that test; disabling collection does not delete that data. The analytics dashboard is private, and there is no public visitor counter. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for Google's processing practices.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical information needed to serve the site under [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Some site resources are delivered through jsDelivr, and external links have their own privacy practices.
