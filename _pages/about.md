I am an M.S. student in Technology Management for Innovation at the Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Tokyo, and a member of the [Matsuo-Iwasawa Laboratory](https://weblab.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/).

My research focuses on **self-improving agent systems and reinforcement learning**.

## Selected research

- **Multi-Axis Max@K Reinforcement Learning** introduces a group-based reinforcement learning objective that improves target-mode coverage in text-to-image generation. Accepted at WACV 2027. [[arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14962)] [[code](https://github.com/KuOnoda/multi-axis-maxk)]
- **Does “Do Differentiable Simulators Give Better Policy Gradients?” Give Better Policy Gradients?** studies gradient estimation in differentiable simulators and practical variance control. Published at ICLR 2026. [[arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18161)]
- **AutoRef** automatically optimizes the agentic harness around frozen image models for multi-reference image generation. [[arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35530)] [[code](https://github.com/KuOnoda/AutoRef)]

For a complete overview, see [Publications](/publications/), [Projects](/projects/), or my [CV](/cv/).
